THE FOUNDER

Who is Chikomborero Walter Sumairi?


Chikomborero Walter Sumairi, popularly known as CWS, is a Zimbabwean Graphic Designer, Creative Director and Entrepreneur born on 26 January 2004. He is the Founder and CEO of CWS Multimedia Connect, a creative media company established in 2020.

His story is one of passion, imagination, determination and purpose.

From a young age, Walter showed a natural passion for art and creativity. Even in the classroom, while lessons were being taught, he would often find himself drawing, sketching and exploring his imagination on paper. What started as a childhood interest gradually developed into a passion that would shape his future and give birth to his dream of becoming a creative professional.

With talent, curiosity and a desire to turn imagination into reality, he began transforming his passion into a purpose. His journey was not simply about learning to design; it was about discovering a gift and finding a way to use it to inspire others.

Like his name, Chikomborero, which means Blessing, Walter sees his journey as a story of grace, resilience and becoming. A fighter with a creative mind, he continues to push boundaries, embrace opportunities and use his craft as a platform to tell stories, connect people and inspire the next generation of creatives.

Today, through CWS Multimedia Connect, he continues to build a brand that represents creativity, innovation and connection.

His story is still being written, and his mission goes beyond design — it’s about making an impact, inspiring others and turning imagination into reality.
